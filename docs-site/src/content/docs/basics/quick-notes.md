Quick notes are standalone comments not tied to a text selection. Use them for thoughts, todos, or feedback that does not quote anything.

## Create a quick note

Create a quick note from the popover or the menu bar icon:

- Click the **New quick note** button in the [popover header](/basics/menu-bar-and-popover/).
- Right-click the menu bar icon and choose **New Quick Note**.

Both open a composer with no quoted text. Write your note and press `Cmd+Return` to save. Quick notes have no global hotkey of their own.

## Quick note behavior

Quick notes behave like any other comment:

- Attach images with the paperclip button or by pasting into the text field.
- Pick a target session with the composer's session picker. See [sessions](/basics/sessions/).
- Cards show a **Quick Note** type label, and support the same statuses and actions as other comments. See [statuses and history](/basics/statuses-and-history/).
